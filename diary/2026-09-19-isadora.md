- ContractHouse is now three parts, the way Isadora described it: the pipeline (dashboard, contracts, payments, calendar), Build a contract, and contract settings (clauses, templates, packages and pricing, import, settings). The nav says so, with a sentence under each group and one on every page.
- Build a contract is one page. It opens on the couples who asked through the website calculator, three of them today, each arriving with their package, date, guests and extras already in. Click one and the left side runs top to bottom: who, package (theirs picked, the others greyed), when (the date sets the season, and the season sets the price), numbers and extras (each add-on brings its clause), terms (the clauses for that package, with greyed ones saying why they are out), payment terms, signing. The right side is the contract as the couple will read it, sticky, rebuilding as you click. Save draft, or Send.
